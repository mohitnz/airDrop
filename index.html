Demo guide

Mock data only. No real payments, OTP or maps.

Try as a Driver

  • Search a destination and time window. Only Spaces free for the whole window and fitting your vehicle show up.
  • Open a Space, book it, enter a number plate and pay with mock UPI.
  • The exact address and access notes appear only after confirmation.
  • In My Bookings: cancel (see refund tier), simulate an Overstay, or report a blocked Space.

Try as a Host

  • Complete mock KYC, then add a Space.
  • Edit the weekly schedule and block dates.
  • See incoming Bookings (with number plates) and report an Overstay.
  • Payouts release 24h after a Booking ends, and are held during disputes.

Pricing rules

  • Host price = hourly rate, capped per day.
  • Service fee = 12% of Host price, plus 18% GST on the fee only.
  • Overstay = 1.5x hourly rate.
